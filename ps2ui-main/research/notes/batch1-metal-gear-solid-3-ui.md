# Metal Gear Solid 3: Snake Eater — PS2 UI/HUD/Menu Aesthetics

## Title Screen

- **Look:** MGS3's title screen evokes 1960s Cold War-era spy aesthetics — a jungle green and earth-tone palette, with a prominent illustrated snake in the logo. The overall color scheme leans toward **olive green** (`~#4a5a30`), **dark jungle green** (`~#2a3820`), and **muted brown** (`~#5a4030`).
- **Type treatment:** "METAL GEAR SOLID 3" in **Helvetica Ultra Compressed** (consistent with the series). The "SNAKE EATER" subtitle is rendered in the same family. The core wordmark uses a "heavily condensed, uppercase style that prioritizes vertical space efficiency, stacking tall letters tightly to create a dense, forceful block of text."
- **Snake illustration:** The logo features a naturalistic snake illustration — not an abstracted icon. This grounds the logo in tangible Cold War-jungle imagery. The snake wraps around or underlies the wordmark in various configurations.
- **Color:** Green shades to reflect the jungle survival setting. The snake itself is rendered in naturalistic olive-brown-green tones. Title type in white/silver on dark green or near-black background.

## Menu / Pause Menu Pattern — The "Survival Viewer"

MGS3 introduced a **redesigned pause menu called the "Survival Viewer"** — a significant departure from MGS2's simpler pause overlay:

- **Function:** Opens a full-screen survival management interface where the player can:
  - Change **camouflage** (face paint + uniform)
  - **Eat food** (captured animals and rations from the jungle)
  - **Treat injuries and illnesses** with medical supplies
  - Manage equipment and weapons
- **Layout:** Panel-based with distinct sections for each survival category. The interface uses jungle/military iconography — earthy textures, military-report formatting.
- **Aesthetic:** Moves away from the clinical phosphor green of MGS2 toward something warmer and more analog — evoking a field manual or military survival handbook. Still monospace/condensed type but with a rougher, more human-made quality.
- **Camouflage selector:** A grid of uniform patterns with percentage effectiveness values. This is the visual heart of MGS3's unique mechanics — camo pattern thumbnails displayed as tactical reference cards.

## In-Game HUD

### Camo Index (Top-Right)
The central MGS3 HUD innovation:
- **Position:** Top-right corner of screen.
- **Display:** A percentage value (0%–100%) showing current camouflage effectiveness.
- **Color coding:**
  - **High effectiveness (safe) — displayed in blue** (`~#4488ff`): 70%+ is sufficient to remain unseen.
  - **Danger zone (low) — displayed in red** (`~#ff3333`): Below ~40%, enemies can easily spot Snake.
  - 100% = theoretical invisibility.

### Life Gauge (Top-Left)
- Horizontal segmented bar — same basic structure as MGS2.
- Green when healthy.

### Stamina Gauge (Below Life Bar)
**New to MGS3** — a major departure from the series:
- Separate bar beneath the life gauge showing Snake's physical stamina/hunger level.
- Affects: running speed, health recovery rate, aiming steadiness.
- **Color change:** Turns **blue** when Snake is underwater (functions as O2 gauge in that context) and decreases faster.
- Also doubles as the **grip gauge** when hanging from surfaces.
- When stamina is depleted: aiming shakes, health does not regenerate, Snake slows.

### Alert States
Inherits MGS2's color-coded alert system:
- NORMAL (no alert), CAUTION, EVASION, ALERT
- Same Helvetica Ultra Compressed status text in matching colors.

### Food/Cure Icons
Small thumbnail icons in the top-right area when relevant — minimalist, military-manual aesthetic.

## Font Character

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Primary logo / HUD | Helvetica Ultra Compressed (series standard, confirmed) |
| Survival Viewer text | Condensed sans, slightly rougher than MGS2's clinical digital — warmer, more analog |
| Camo index readout | Monospace-style digits; large, legible |
| Alert/status text | Helvetica Ultra Compressed, all caps |
| Weight | Ultra-compressed; no weight variation |
| Tracking | Same as MGS2: compressed horizontal, minimal letter-spacing |
| Character | Military survival / field-manual aesthetic — rougher than MGS2's clean digital |

## Color Palette / Grading

| Name | Approx Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Jungle Black | `#0a0c08` | Background (darker than MGS2, warmer) |
| Olive Green | `#4a5a30` | Title screen, jungle environment tones |
| Dark Jungle | `#2a3820` | Deep shadow areas |
| Earth Brown | `#5a4030` | Logo snake tones, warm accents |
| Camo Blue (safe index) | `#4488ff` | Camo % when effective |
| Danger Red (low index) | `#ff3333` | Camo % when dangerously low |
| Life Green | `#33cc44` | Life gauge normal state |
| Stamina Blue (water) | `#2266ff` | Stamina gauge when underwater |
| White / Silver | `#e8e8e8` | Primary text |

**Overall grading:** MGS3 shifts from MGS2's cold clinical green-phosphor to a warmer, jungle-inflected palette. The background blacks have a warmer undertone (vs. MGS2's cold grey-black). Earth greens and browns replace the sterile industrial tones, while retaining the series' functional signal-color system (blue=safe, red=danger).

## Cross-Reference Notes

- MGS3 represents the "survival" iteration of the series' UI philosophy: the Survival Viewer is the most complex pause-menu system in the series, yet retains the compressed-sans military aesthetic.
- The camouflage percentage display is a masterstroke of contextual UI — a single number that encodes spatial, tactical, and decision information with clear color feedback.
- Helvetica Ultra Compressed has been used across the series continuously from Metal Gear 2 (1990) through Peace Walker (2010). The series never departed from this core typographic identity on PS2.
