# Devil May Cry (2001, PS2) — UI / HUD / Menu Aesthetics

## Title Screen

- **Color**: Near-black with deep crimson red accents. The DMC title screen opens with atmospheric darkness — stone, shadow, and blood-red. The "DEVIL MAY CRY" wordmark appears in a heavily stylized gothic/calligraphic display font on a near-black or very dark red-brown ground.
- **Type treatment**: The DMC title logo uses a custom calligraphic display face with strong blackletter / Art Nouveau / gothic influences — organic, flowing, yet bold. Community font IDs (dafont forums) suggest similarities to fonts in the **Trajan** / gothic calligraphy family. The in-game menu font (identified in dafont discussions) is **Octin Spraypaint Free** or a similar spray-stencil display font for some elements — gritty, textured, masculine.
- **Visual references**: The development team took a research trip to Europe (UK and Spain) photographing Gothic architecture, statues, stone pavements — these textures directly informed the aesthetic. The title screen aesthetic is Gothic revival + action-movie poster.
- **Motion**: Dramatic red-to-black wipe; Dante character reveal; hard impact cuts (not soft fades like Square games). High contrast, cinematic pacing.

## Mission Select / Pause Menu

- **Layout**: Mission select screens use a dark stone-textured background with a semi-transparent dark panel overlay. Menu items are listed vertically in bold, spaced sans-serif or stencil-style type — white or pale grey on dark.
- **Panel style**: Very dark, near-opaque panels (not the translucent blue of Square JRPGs). The "window" metaphor is absent — menus feel more like tactical mission briefings or stone tablets. Near-opaque black or charcoal overlays, `approx #0A0806` at 90%+ opacity.
- **Style meter display**: Unique to DMC — the style rating (D, C, B, A, S, SS, SSS) appears in large bold all-caps type, upper-left or upper-center, in a strong accent color (white or gold). This is the most cinematically prominent HUD element — it broadcasts the player's performance theatrically.
- **Font**: Mission titles and style ratings use a bold condensed grotesque sans or stencil display — high impact, tight tracking in caps. The menu body font is a clean bold sans (similar to Impact or a condensed grotesque). **Octin Spraypaint** confirmed for some menu elements via community ID.

## HUD (In-Level)

- **Vitality Gauge (HP)**: Upper-left corner. A solid horizontal bar — not segmented with slits in DMC1 (unlike later games). Full bar = green. As HP depletes, the bar shortens leftward. When life is restored, a blue-colored portion rises up to show the recovery. No decorative border — the bar is utilitarian, industrial.
- **Devil Trigger Gauge**: Directly below the Vitality Gauge. Displayed as a **line of glowing rune symbols** — each rune represents one unit of DT energy. This is not a smooth bar but a series of separate glyph-like icons. Extremely distinctive — the only PS2-era HUD element that uses actual iconographic glyphs (not geometry) as the gauge units.
- **Red Orb counter**: Bottom-right corner. A large red gem icon with a numeric counter alongside — the currency accumulation display. Bold white numerals, red gem icon in a slightly baroque illustrated style.
- **Style Rank**: Upper-center or upper-left, very large — white bold all-caps letter grades. Changes with combo performance. The most attention-commanding element in the HUD.
- **Mission title cards**: When entering a new room or triggering a cutscene, a title card appears — gothic calligraphic text over a dark transitional overlay, like a theatrical title card.

## Font Character

- **Logo**: Custom gothic calligraphic / Art Nouveau display — organic, flowing, high contrast stroke modulation. Not a standard system font.
- **In-menu body**: Bold condensed grotesque sans (Impact-adjacent or **Octin Spraypaint**) — stencil/military aesthetic, tight tracking.
- **Style rank display**: Heavy bold condensed uppercase — theatrical legibility at large display sizes.
- **Tracking**: Tight to medium. The dark-on-dark aesthetic requires strong type weight to maintain legibility — thin fonts would disappear.
- **Serif vs sans**: Title is calligraphic/display (not strictly serif); menus are sans/stencil. The combination creates a gothic-action-game feel: decorative for branding, utilitarian for information.

## Design Signature

Devil May Cry's UI is **gothic action theater**. The HUD is built for drama: the style meter broadcasts player virtuosity at large scale; the rune-glyph DT gauge is literally iconographic, not just a progress bar. The near-black, near-opaque menus feel like stone tablets or dark iron — completely opposite to Square's translucent blue glass. The red orb currency symbol is baroque and jeweled. The overall palette — near-black + crimson red + violet DT runes + white style letters — maps directly to the gothic horror + action-hero aesthetic. Unlike Persona or FF, DMC never uses its UI to carry narrative theme; instead the HUD is designed for **performance readability** — everything serves knowing your style rank.
